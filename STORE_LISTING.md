## Google Play Store

**App name:** ClosetAI

**Short description** (max 80 characters):
> AI wardrobe stylist: organize clothes, plan outfits, and never repeat.

**Full description:**

ClosetAI is your AI-powered personal stylist and wardrobe manager. Snap a photo of any clothing item and let AI detect its category, color, and style — then get outfit recommendations tailored to the occasion, weather, and season.

**Key features:**
- 📸 AI clothing detection — photograph an item and ClosetAI fills in the details
- 🤖 AI outfit recommendations based on occasion, weather, temperature, and season
- 👗 Full wardrobe management — search, filter by category/color/season/brand/size
- 📅 Outfit calendar and wear history — plan ahead and track what you've worn
- 🧺 Laundry tracker — items in the wash are automatically excluded from recommendations
- ✈️ Trip planner with AI-assisted packing checklists
- 📊 Wardrobe analytics — most/least worn items, category breakdowns, wear frequency
- 🔔 Smart reminders for laundry, planned outfits, and upcoming trips
- ⭐ Premium plans for unlimited AI recommendations and advanced analytics

Your data stays yours: clothing photos are securely stored, and you're always in control of your wardrobe data.

**App category:** Lifestyle

**Contact information:** support@closetai.app

**Data safety information (summary for the Play Console form):**
- Data collected: account info (name, email), photos (clothing images), app activity (wardrobe/outfit/laundry/trip usage)
- Data shared with third parties for service delivery only: Cloudinary (image hosting), AI provider (image/outfit analysis), Razorpay (payments), MongoDB Atlas (database hosting)
- Data is encrypted in transit
- Users can request account/data deletion (see Privacy Policy)
- No data is sold to third parties; no third-party advertising SDKs are included

**Content rating:** Everyone / suitable for general audiences (no user-generated public content, no violence, no mature themes)

## Apple App Store

ClosetAI turns your closet into a smart, searchable wardrobe. Photograph any item and AI automatically detects its category, color, and style. Tell ClosetAI the occasion, weather, and season, and it recommends a complete outfit from clothes you already own.
